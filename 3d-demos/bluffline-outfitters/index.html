The Mill Bridge float

9 miles, about 4 hours with a lunch stop.

  1. Put-in behind the shopMile 0

    We help you load up and push off. The first mile is slow, flat water to get used to steering.

  2. Cold Spring bendMile 3

    A spring comes out of the bluff here. The water is cold all summer and clear enough to see the bottom.

  3. Gravel bar lunchMile 5.5

    A wide, shaded gravel bar, the best spot to pull out, eat, and swim.

  4. Take-out at Mill BridgeMile 9

    Our van waits at the landing and drives you back to your car.
